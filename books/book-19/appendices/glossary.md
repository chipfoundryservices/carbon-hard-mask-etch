# Glossary: Carbon Hard Mask Etch Terminology

---

## A

**ARDE (Aspect Ratio Dependent Etching):** Variation in etch rate with trench depth and feature size; top of trenches etch faster than bottom due to ion depletion and neutral shadowing.

**Activation Energy (E_a):** Energy barrier for a chemical reaction; determines temperature sensitivity of etch rate via Arrhenius relation.

---

## B

**Bias Power (W_bias):** RF power applied to wafer electrode (typically 2 MHz); controls ion energy delivered to wafer surface.

**Blister (Delamination):** Carbon film peeling away from substrate due to mechanical stress or adhesion failure.

---

## C

**CCP (Capacitive Coupling Plasma):** Plasma generated by RF voltage applied directly between electrodes; electron and ion generation via capacitive field.

**CF₄ (Carbon Tetrafluoride):** Fluorine-containing gas commonly used for carbon etch; dissociates into fluorine radicals in plasma.

**Charge Trap NAND:** 3D NAND architecture using trapped charge in Si₃N₄ layer instead of floating gate for data storage.

**Cluster Tool:** Integrated wafer processing system with multiple process chambers in sequence; handles 50-200 wafers/hour on 300mm platforms.

**Coil Power (W_coil):** RF power applied to inductive coil (typically 13.56 MHz); generates bulk plasma via magnetic field.

---

## D

**Debye Sheath:** Thin region near electrode/wall where electric field accelerates ions; defines boundary between plasma and surfaces.

---

## E

**Electrode:** Conductive surface in plasma chamber; typically powered RF at one end, grounded at other.

**Endpoint Detection:** In-situ sensor measurement (optical or electrical) to determine when etch reaches target depth.

---

## F

**F· (Fluorine Radical):** Atomic fluorine generated by plasma dissociation; primary reactive species attacking carbon.

**FWHM (Full Width at Half Maximum):** Width of ion energy distribution at 50% peak intensity; measure of distribution breadth.

**Fluorocarbon Polymer:** CₓFᵧ compound formed by polymerization of CF radicals; accumulates on surfaces during etch, provides passivation.

---

## H

**Hard Mask:** Etch-resistant material (carbon, SiO₂, Si₃N₄) used to transfer photolithographic pattern into device layers.

---

## I

**ICP (Inductive Coupling Plasma):** Plasma generated by RF coil outside chamber; high electron temperature and density via magnetic confinement.

**Ion Energy:** Kinetic energy of ions striking wafer surface (typically 20-150 eV); controls sputtering yield and selectivity.

**Ion Energy Distribution (IED):** Statistical distribution of ion energies reaching wafer; typically Maxwell-Boltzmann, peak ~70 eV typical.

---

## L

**L-match (Impedance Matching Network):** Simple matching network with inductor and capacitor in L-shape; limited tuning range.

---

## M

**Mean Free Path (MFP):** Average distance between molecular collisions; determines ballistic vs. diffusive transport regime.

**Microloading:** Feature-size-dependent etch rate; small features in dense regions etch slower due to radical depletion.

---

## N

**Neutral Radical:** Fluorine atom (F·) or other reactive species without net charge; travels ballistically in low-pressure plasma.

---

## O

**Oxide Overlayer:** Thin SiO₂ or SiOₓ layer on carbon or silicon surface formed during oxidation; blocks F· attack.

---

## P

**Pattern Placement Error (PPE):** Deviation of feature position from target during etch; caused by thermal distortion or thermal transients.

**Phase Space:** Multi-dimensional parameter space (pressure, power, temperature) defining process operating region.

**π-match (Impedance Matching Network):** Matching network with two capacitors and inductor in π-shape; industry standard, wider tuning range.

**Plasma:** Ionized gas containing electrons, ions, and neutral species; 4th state of matter.

**Plasma Density:** Number of electrons or ions per unit volume; affects etch rate and uniformity.

**Pressure Uniformity:** Variation of chamber pressure across wafer surface; target ±5% for good uniformity.

---

## R

**Reflected Power:** RF power returned from plasma to generator; caused by impedance mismatch; target <5% of forward power.

**RPA (Retarding Potential Analyzer):** Device measuring ion energy distribution by scanning retarding potential; diagnostic tool.

**Roughness (Ra):** Arithmetic mean of surface height deviations; increases during etch from ion sputtering and polymer unevenness.

---

## S

**Selectivity:** Ratio of etch rates between target (carbon) and underlayer (SiO₂); target 10-20:1 typical.

**Self-bias Voltage (V_bias):** Negative DC voltage that develops on powered electrode due to charge imbalance; accelerates ions.

**Showerhead:** Gas distribution system above wafer; multiple small orifices create uniform radical flux.

**sp² Bonding:** Trigonal planar carbon bonding (graphite-like); lower bond strength than sp³, more reactive.

**sp³ Bonding:** Tetrahedral carbon bonding (diamond-like); strong bonds, less reactive.

**Sputtering Yield (Y):** Number of atoms removed per incident ion; depends on ion energy and species.

---

## T

**Thermal Conductivity (k):** Rate of heat transfer through material; carbon ~50 W/m·K, much lower than silicon ~150 W/m·K.

**Thermal Distortion:** Dimensional change due to temperature variation; CTE mismatch creates stress.

**Thermal Management:** Active control of wafer temperature via cooling system; critical for ±2°C uniformity.

**Thermal Uniformity:** Spatial variation of wafer temperature; target ±2-3°C across 300mm diameter.

**Thermalization:** Process of reaching thermal equilibrium; 30-60 second time constant typical for 300mm wafers.

---

## V

**Voltage Standing Wave Ratio (VSWR):** Measure of impedance match; VSWR <1.5:1 target for efficient power transfer.

---

## W

**Wafer Temperature:** Substrate temperature during etch; affects etch rate exponentially (~13%/10°C), selectivity, and polymer formation.

---

This glossary covers primary terminology used throughout Book #19. Terms are defined in production context with typical numerical values where applicable.
